Subject: [tilelang-mlir-ascend] A2 rebase onto tilelang v0.1.15 — status

Hello,

The Ascend 910 (A2) backend has been rebased onto tilelang v0.1.15 on the
`rebase-v0.1.15` branch:

https://github.com/CeleNewYear/tilelang-mlir-ascend/tree/rebase-v0.1.15

Highlights:
- Parallel `src/ascend910` + `tilelang/ascend910` implementation, independent
  of the Ascend 950 backend.
- Legacy A2 frontend restored through `tilelang.language` (Kernel/copy/alloc,
  npuir ops, gemm, Scope, sync, reduce).
- Golden corpus committed; 26/34 migrated examples reach canonicalized MLIR,
  11 match golden byte-for-byte (vec_add 1D/2D/dynamic, atomic add, exp2/log2,
  rms norm, fp8 indexer, ...).
- `tilelang.compile(target="ascend910")` compiles MLIR to `kernel.o` and
  `libkernel_launcher.so` with a content-addressed cache.

Remaining:
- Device load/launch runtime bridge (needs NPU host).
- Mixed Cube/Vector codegen for flash attention and sparse MLA.
- Autotune profiling and CI runners.

Full details: docs/ascend910/verification.md

Best regards
